# DSA Restart Plan

No fixed interview date — the goal right now is knocking rust off, not cramming. Once the pattern buckets below feel solid, layer in the "Top Google Questions" list as phase 2 (tracker for that can come later — no point tracking a list you're not ready to draw signal from yet).

One easy/medium problem per bucket, no clock. Just note what came back on its own vs. what felt foreign. This tells you where phase 1 should actually start — don't just go in bucket order below.

## Phase 1 — Rebuild reflexes (target: ~2–3 relaxed weeks)

Work the buckets that came back "slow/blank" first. A handful of easy→medium problems per bucket, ~30–40 min each, loosely timed — finish each one before moving on. Goal is the recognition reflex, not volume.

## Phase 2 — Google-specific list (not yet)

Once phase 1 buckets feel solid, bring in the "Top Google Questions" list (50 problems: 1 Easy / 31 Medium / 18 Hard) as its own tracker — most mediums should already map onto a rebuilt pattern by then, so the work becomes the Google-specific twist and speed under time pressure, plus the hards. Mock interviews (explaining out loud, clarifying requirements, stating complexity) should start running alongside this phase, not bolted on at the end.
